# Technical references

Primary references used to frame this engineering model:

1. C. I. Calle et al., **“Electrodynamic Dust Shield for Surface Exploration
   Activities on the Moon and Mars.”** NASA Technical Reports Server,
   document 20130012545.
   https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20130012545

2. C. I. Calle et al., **“Numerical and Analytical Model of an Electrodynamic
   Dust Shield for Solar Panels on Mars.”** NASA Technical Reports Server,
   document 20130011382.
   https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20130011382

3. C. I. Calle et al., **“Active Dust Control and Mitigation Technology for
   Lunar and Martian Exploration.”** NASA Technical Reports Server,
   document 20100036405.
   https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20100036405

4. C. I. Calle et al., **“History and Flight Development of the
   Electrodynamic Dust Shield.”** NASA Technical Reports Server,
   document 20150016082.
   https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20150016082

5. NASA Kennedy Space Center, **Electrostatics and Surface Physics
   Laboratory.**
   https://www.nasa.gov/exploration-research-and-technology/electrostatics-and-surface-physics-laboratory/

6. NASA Space Logistics Technology Catalog, **Dust Mitigation /
   Electrodynamic Dust Shield.**
   https://www.nasa.gov/dust-mitigation/

NASA's literature establishes the EDS technology heritage, the use of
electrostatic and dielectrophoretic forces, multiphase traveling fields, and
the importance of avoiding low-pressure electrical breakdown. It does not
validate the numerical outputs of this repository.
